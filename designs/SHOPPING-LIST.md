## Suggested "smart money" build (best value-for-look)

- Desk: Flexispot walnut + frame
- Chair: **Sihoo Doro C300** (headrest, looks premium on camera)
- Walls: Naturewall/Acupanel slats on the backdrop wall + matte black paint on faced wall
- Lights: **2× Elgato Key Light** (the cleanest look + app control) + **BenQ ScreenBar**
- Mic: **Shure MV7+** (USB — no interface needed)
- Camera: **Sony ZV-E10 II** + 16–50, on **Elgato Master Mount**
- Monitors: LG 27" QHD + 24" pivot on **NB F160** arm
- Keyboard/mouse: **Keychron K2** + **Logitech MX Master 3S**
- Desk: **Orbitkey desk mat + Nest**, Lamicall tablet stand, walnut pen cup
- Mood: **Govee RGBIC floor lamp** in the corner + warm LED strip behind shelf

**≈ $2,800–3,200** for a studio that looks like the render.
